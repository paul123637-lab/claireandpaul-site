How it started

We met on Hinge in February 2021. Paul was home in Texas taking care of his mom through the pandemic while working on his MPH, and Claire was in her last few years of college.

After a few weeks of talking, Paul flew Claire out to Texas, and Claire took a leap of faith and came to see him for a weekend. The rest is history.

Together since February 21, 2021

It's been more than five years, and we've been through everything together. When Paul's beautiful mom, Wendy, passed away after her brave battle with stage IV lung cancer, Claire was right there.

There have been plenty of adventures too: Disney World and Disneyland, Dallas (our second home), Houston and Austin, Albuquerque, three EDCs in Las Vegas, Coachella, Big Bear, Korea, and more San Diego and Irvine weekends than we can count. See them on the map.

We love the Lord, and we're so grateful to walk together in faith as partners for life.

Flying in

Fallbrook is in North San Diego County, between San Diego and Orange County. San Diego (SAN), John Wayne (SNA), Ontario (ONT), and Los Angeles (LAX) are airport options. Compare flights with the drive to Fallbrook in mind, and allow extra time for traffic.

We'd recommend renting a car. If you're getting a ride, book your ride home ahead of time, since the venue is out in the hills.

Where to stay

Temecula
A nearby option, about 20 minutes away. Old Town is fun to walk around, and wine country is right there.
Oceanside & Carlsbad
About half an hour away, right on the beach. Great if you want ocean mornings.
Irvine
About an hour north, and easy if you fly into John Wayne. Clean, central, and full of good food.
San Diego
About an hour south. Pick this one if you want to make a whole trip of it.

These drive times are approximate and depend on traffic and your exact location.
