How it started

Who would've thought that a little dating app called Hinge would lead us all the way to the altar? Our story began in January 2021, during a time when the world was a little upside down! Claire was living in California, Paul was in Texas, and somehow, despite being over a thousand miles apart, they found each other. What started as a few messages on Hinge quickly turned into hours of conversations, late nights on Discord, and slowly becoming each other's favorite person to talk to.

With COVID and Paul's mom battling lung cancer at the time, meeting in person wasn't exactly easy. But even from a distance, Paul always found ways to make Claire feel special. Before they had even met, he asked her to be exclusive by surprising her with champagne and dinner delivered to her door. (Yes, he was already setting the bar pretty high.)

A few months later, Paul bought Claire a plane ticket to Lubbock, Texas, so they could finally meet in person. And let's just say Paul was a little nervous. When Claire finally saw him at the airport, he was exactly the same person she'd gotten to know online... just a lot taller! Meanwhile, Paul was so nervous that while standing at baggage claim, he asked Claire, "Did you check in your bags?" even though she had already landed. HAHAHA.

But despite the nerves, meeting in person only confirmed what they already knew. Something about this relationship was worth every mile. They officially began dating, and what followed was over five years of long distance. Years filled with flights, airport goodbyes, late-night calls, counting down the days until the next visit, and learning how to love each other from two different states. Through it all, they continued choosing each other.

Then, on October 18, 2025, Paul planned a proposal in Palos Verdes, a place that held a special meaning for him and his mom. He and his best man, Joe, had planned everything well in advance. But apparently, no amount of preparation could save Paul from his nerves, because he somehow ended up going the wrong way!

And as if that wasn't enough, Claire spotted a very familiar car parked nearby. With a My Melody sticker and an Eastside Church sticker, she was almost certain it belonged to her best friend, Loise. When she brought it up, Paul confidently told her it wasn't her car and even suggested they go check. Somehow, Claire believed him and dropped the subject. (Turns out Paul is a surprisingly good liar when there's a proposal on the line.)

Despite a few nervous detours, everything worked out beautifully, and Paul asked Claire to marry him. Of course, she said yes!

On August 21, 2026, after more than five years of long distance, Claire finally made the move to Texas. No more counting down the days until the next flight. No more airport goodbyes. And no more wishing they lived a little closer.

Looking back, we truly believe God brought us together at just the right time. What started with a simple swipe grew into a love built on patience, faith, countless miles, and a whole lot of laughter.

And after all those years of saying goodbye, we finally get today "I do."

We can't wait to celebrate this next chapter with all of you!

Flying in

Fallbrook is in North San Diego County, between San Diego and Orange County. San Diego (SAN), John Wayne (SNA), Ontario (ONT), and Los Angeles (LAX) are airport options. Compare flights with the drive to Fallbrook in mind, and allow extra time for traffic.

We'd recommend renting a car. If you're getting a ride, book your ride home ahead of time, since the venue is out in the hills.

Where to stay

Temecula
A nearby option, about 20 minutes away. Old Town is fun to walk around, and wine country is right there.
Oceanside & Carlsbad
About half an hour away, right on the beach. Great if you want ocean mornings.
Irvine
About an hour north, and easy if you fly into John Wayne. Clean, central, and full of good food.
San Diego
About an hour south. Pick this one if you want to make a whole trip of it.

These drive times are approximate and depend on traffic and your exact location.
